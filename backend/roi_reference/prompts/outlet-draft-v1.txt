Annotate rainwater outlet symbol extents clearly serving the supplied accepted roof ROIs. Use subtype internal_outlet, parapet_outlet, or scupper and associate every object with one supplied roi_id. Include boundary symbols and immediately adjacent symbols when drawing evidence establishes that they serve that roof. Exclude unrelated RWP labels, downpipes without an identifiable roof outlet, text-only label boxes, and outlets serving other roofs. Do not treat a label centroid as the outlet position or infer diameter or drainage design. This is a draft category policy for evaluation, awaiting domain review.
