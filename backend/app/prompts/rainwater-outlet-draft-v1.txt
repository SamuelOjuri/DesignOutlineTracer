Annotate rainwater outlet symbol extents clearly serving the supplied accepted roof ROIs. Use subtype internal_outlet, parapet_outlet, or scupper and associate every object with one supplied roi_id. Include boundary and immediately adjacent symbols when drawing evidence establishes that they serve that roof. Exclude unrelated RWP labels, downpipes without identifiable roof outlets, text-only label boxes, and outlets serving other roofs. Do not use label centroids as outlet positions or infer diameters, falls, edge assignments, or hydraulic design. This category policy is a draft pending domain review.
