Annotate rooflight, vent, flue, and access_hatch object/symbol extents belonging to the supplied accepted roof ROIs. Use exactly those subtype values and associate every object with one supplied roi_id. Use actual roof scope and drawing evidence, not bounding-box containment alone; exclude objects in empty portions of a nonrectangular roof's box. Exclude arbitrary equipment, text labels, drainage outlets, and out-of-scope objects. Interior rooflights are penetration annotations, not roof ROI detections. Do not infer physical dimensions or holes. This category policy is a draft pending domain review.
